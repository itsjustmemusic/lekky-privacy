Lekky privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

App: Lekky (Android), package com.lekky.app

Developer: Lekky

Contact: lekkyapp@gmail.com

The short version

Lekky keeps your data on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your readings, top-ups or settings.

What Lekky stores

On your phone only:

  • the meter readings, top-ups, notes and gap records you enter;
  • your settings (language, currency, alerts, reminders, quiet hours, display choices);
  • a copy of your data made before a restore, so you can undo it, and a copy made before a database upgrade.

This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is not included in Android's automatic Google Drive backup, so uninstalling Lekky deletes it. To keep it, use Settings › Backup & restore › Back up to save a backup file wherever you choose, and Restore to bring it back.

When Lekky uses the internet

Lekky asks for internet access for one thing: Settings › About › Check for updates. When you tap it, Lekky asks the Google Play Store whether a newer version exists. Google receives the request as it does for any visit to the Play Store. Nothing from your data is sent.

Lekky also opens other apps when you ask it to:

  • Email Support and Lekky Pro: tell me which you'd like most open your own email app with a message ready to send. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. A support email may include the Lekky version, Android version and phone model, so problems can be fixed; you can delete these before sending. Emails you send are handled under the privacy terms of your email provider, and the developer uses them only to reply and improve Lekky.
  • Donate opens PayPal in your browser. Any donation is handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy; Lekky never sees your payment details.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): low balance, top-up and reading reminder alerts.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, optional): so alerts arrive on time.
  • Run at start-up (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to set alerts again after the phone restarts.
  • Wake lock and vibration (WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE): to show alerts and give a short vibration on taps (this can be turned off).
  • Internet (INTERNET): only for Check for updates.

Lekky does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage. Backup files are saved and opened through Android's own file picker, and meter photos come from Android's own camera app or photo picker.

Reading the meter from a photo

In Add Entry, Read from a photo lets you take or choose a photo of the meter's screen. Android's own camera app or photo picker provides the photo, so Lekky needs no camera or storage permission. Lekky reads the numbers on the phone itself; the photo is never sent anywhere, and Lekky deletes its copy when you leave that screen. The number is only saved if you choose to use it and then save the entry. When you use a reading, Lekky keeps small cut-outs of its digits (not the photo), on this phone only, to read your meter better next time; Forget what Lekky learned (in Settings › Meter › Read from a photo), or Delete All Data, deletes them.

Sharing and selling

Lekky does not collect personal data, so there is nothing to share or sell.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Backup & restore › Delete All Data (this also deletes the copies Lekky keeps), or uninstall Lekky. Backup files you saved are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children

Lekky is meant for adults managing a household electricity meter and is not directed at children.
